# Purpose-shaped handoff probes

These two short drafts reuse exactly the saved Valkey/Redis evidence and dossier from `../../valkey-redis-editorial/research/`. They test an author’s ability to select structure and emphasis from the supplied studio task. They are not independently reviewed output bundles, full blog posts or training lessons, and are not evidence of broad reliability across topics.

- [Blog task](blog.md): develops one argument, its mechanism and a material objection.
- [Introductory training task](introductory-training.md): orders concepts, exposes a misconception and supplies a hypothetical worked-example ingredient.
- [Presentation handoff](../../valkey-redis-editorial/research/brief.md): prioritizes a central message, three-step storyline, speaker-note evidence and must-retain limits. Its independent review state is reported in that case’s manifest.

Author: `/root/editorial_example` (`editorial-author`). Date limits: original captures accessed 2026-10-17; no fresh browsing, benchmarks, lab work or production tests. The dossier and evidence links retain source/fact references, exact quotes and their qualifications. This is editorial reuse rather than a research-speed benchmark.
